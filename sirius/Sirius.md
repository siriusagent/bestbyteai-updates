# Sirius 0.1.8.22 · Build 263

## Gemini setup

- **Use an API key** links to Google AI Studio and checks your key against a live Gemini model before saving it. Retry a failed check without losing your draft or replacing your current connection.
- **Sign in with Google** now completes the browser handoff and can renew access after the original token expires. After connecting, choose a model from the roster available to your Google account.
- **Google OAuth verification is pending.** Google may show an unverified-app warning or limit new sign-ins while it reviews Sirius. API-key setup is available in **Settings → Network & Providers → Connections → Google Gemini**.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
