# Sirius 0.1.5

## A first look at the new Sirius

Sirius is becoming a workspace for getting things done on your Mac. Your
conversation, browser, terminal and files live together, so you can follow the
work from the first request through to the finished result.

This release is a preview of that experience on the way to 0.2.0.

## Keep the whole task in view

- Browse the web beside your conversation in the built-in browser.
- Open the terminal, inspect changes in DiffTree, and read generated files
  without leaving the workspace.
- Move between your tools and documents with the shared tab bar and navigation.
- Open a Temporary Chat for a separate side conversation.

## Your files have a home

Library brings saved attachments and files from your conversations into one
place. Preview them, find their source, and bring them into your next chat.

Deleting a chat now keeps its saved files in Library. Recent Files and View
Library lead back to the same file, including files whose original chat is gone.

## Follow the work without the clutter

Terminal commands and status checks now collect into expandable activity groups.
You can see what happened at a glance, then open any command to inspect its
output or error.

Long conversations also recover earlier requests and decisions more reliably
after context compression. Better handling of active provider requests reduces
interruptions during longer waits.

## Start with a task

Choose your preferred model and try a task: research a question, inspect a
codebase, or create something you can keep in Library.

Requires macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.
